Bring the players.
Take 5% of the profit.

Every player you bring to Young Money earns you a cut of the gross profit they generate. No credits, no ladders. Real money, paid every Monday. And the top 3 every month drive away with more.

Every Monday we total your players' week and send you 5% of the gross profit.

THE MATH

One formula. No fine print.

Your players' deposits− their cashouts= gross profit× 5%

Example: your players deposit $7,450 this week and cash out $4,450. That's $3,000 gross profit, so you get $150 on Monday.

The rules

Paid every Monday

Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Payouts go out the following Monday, by your usual payment method.

Losing weeks carry forward

If your players win more than they deposit, you get $0 that week and the loss comes off your next winning week. The balance stays until your players' profit covers it.

$[MIN] minimum payout

Weeks under the minimum roll into the next payout. Nothing is lost.

Your players stay yours

You earn on every player you bring for as long as you stay an active affiliate. Builders from the old Credit Ladder keep all their players.

Your own play doesn't count

Commission comes only from players you referred. One account per person: second accounts are removed and their commission is cancelled.

Who can join

Anyone can join with their own unique email and phone number. Ask your Young Money rep for your OG code.

ID check on big payouts

Payouts over $[AMOUNT] need a quick ID check before we send. The name on your ID must match your account.

Business rules

  • Commission: 5% of weekly gross profit from the affiliate's referred players. Gross profit = verified deposits − verified cashouts.
  • Week: Monday–Sunday, paid the following Monday.
  • Losing weeks: commission is $0 and the negative amount carries forward against future winning weeks. It never resets.
  • Minimum payout: $10. Smaller amounts roll into the next payout.
  • Who counts: only referred players, never the affiliate's own play. Players stay linked to the affiliate for as long as the affiliate is active.
  • Signup: anyone can become an affiliate. Email and phone must be unique across all affiliates and players.
  • ID check: required before any payout over $[AMOUNT] and before releasing any Top 3 prize. The name on the ID must match the account.
  • Top 3 race: monthly (1st to last day of the month), ranked by total gross profit from referred players. 1st place wins a car, 2nd a dirt bike, 3rd an iPhone, on top of the 5%. Flagged accounts are excluded and the next affiliate moves up.
  • Status badges (Builder → OG Status): recognition only, based on last month's earnings. Thresholds are TBD.
  • Transactions: a player who signs up through an affiliate link is auto-tagged, then an employee confirms the link. Every deposit/cashout entry needs the amount, payment method, transaction ID and a screenshot.
  • Two-person rule: a second employee must verify each entry before it counts, and nobody can verify their own entry. Rejections need a reason. Only an ops manager can edit an entry after it's verified.
  • Flags: a player whose email, phone or payment handle matches their affiliate's account is a likely self-referral and goes to ops for review.
