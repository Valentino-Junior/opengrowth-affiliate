The rules

Paid every Monday

Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Payouts go out the following Monday, by your usual payment method.

Losing weeks carry forward

If your players win more than they deposit, you get $0 that week and the loss comes off your next winning week. The balance stays until your players' profit covers it.
